# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Young Kenyans (18–35), specifically voters and youth leaders in Makina Ward, Kibra Constituency, and nationwide. They are digital-native but often access the site on mobile devices in varied lighting conditions.

## Product Purpose
Campaign platform for Richard Miruka's National Youth Council (NYC) 2026 candidacy. The site aims to mobilize youth, provide accountability through data-driven pillars (ICT, Skills, Capital, Policy, Wellness), and drive voter registration and turnout for the July 5th, 2026 election.

## Brand Personality
Grassroots, authoritative, empowered. The brand reflects a leader who is "one of us" but possesses the professional and technical expertise to represent youth at a national level. It balances the energy of a movement with the stability of a strategic policy platform.

## Anti-references
- Generic political templates with loud, clashing primary colors.
- Low-contrast, "aesthetic" gray-on-white typography that fails in outdoor lighting.
- Static, uninspiring layouts that don't reflect the "Digital Superhighway" vision.

## Design Principles
- **Readability First**: High-contrast typography and clear visual hierarchy to ensure legibility in all environments (outdoor/indoor).
- **Data-Backed Authority**: Use stats and clear pillars to ground campaign promises in reality.
- **Youth-Centric Motion**: Use purposeful, high-quality animations (Framer Motion) to feel "alive" and modern without being overstimulating.
- **Mobile-First Accessibility**: Optimize for the primary device of the youth demographic, ensuring fast loads and touch-friendly interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast**: Strict adherence to WCAG AA/AAA for text contrast to support reading in bright sunlight.
- **Motion**: Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all Framer Motion interactions.
- **Structure**: Semantic HTML for screen reader compatibility, especially for the "Pillars" and "Stats" sections.
